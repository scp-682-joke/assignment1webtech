Markiplier

Content Creator, Actor and Filmmaker

About Markiplier

Mark Edward Fischbach, better known as Markiplier, was born in Honolulu, Hawaii, in 1989. He began creating videos on YouTube in 2012 and became especially well known for playing horror and independent video games. Over time, he expanded his work beyond gaming into acting, writing, directing, and producing. His creative projects have helped him become one of the most recognizable content creators on YouTube.

Important Events

  1. 1989 – Mark Edward Fischbach was born in Honolulu, Hawaii.
  2. 2012 – He began his YouTube career under the name Markiplier.
  3. 2015 – His channel reached 10 million subscribers.
  4. 2019 – He released the interactive YouTube project A Heist with Markiplier.
  5. 2022 – He released another interactive project, In Space with Markiplier.
  6. 2026 – He made his theatrical directing debut with the horror film Iron Lung.
